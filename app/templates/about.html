About ASPEN

Algorithmic Spoilage‑Prevention Engine – A KNUST CS Mini‑Project

The Problem

In Ghana, a significant portion of harvested perishable crops spoils at the farm level before ever reaching a buyer. This post‑harvest loss is not due to a lack of demand – restaurants, processors, and retailers exist – but because of a critical coordination failure. Traditional marketplaces treat all produce equally, ignore the ticking spoilage clock, and rely on manual searching.

Our Solution

ASPEN is a full‑stack web application that algorithmically minimizes post‑harvest food waste by automatically matching perishable agricultural supply with nearby demand, giving priority to produce that is closest to spoiling. It models each crop's allocation as a minimum‑cost flow problem, using graph theory and operations research to save food before time runs out.
