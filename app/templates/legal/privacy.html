Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register and use ASPEN, we collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Account role (farmer or vendor)
  • Region, district, and town/city
  • GPS coordinates (if you choose to share them)
  • Produce details (crop type, quantity, spoilage time)
  • SMS logs for notifications

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Match supply with demand using our algorithm
  • Send SMS notifications about matches and status updates
  • Provide admin oversight and dispute resolution
  • Improve the platform for educational evaluation

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your information is shared only with:

  • The matched farmer or vendor involved in a transaction
  • Platform administrators for oversight and audit
  • SMS gateway providers for delivering notifications

4. Storage and Retention

Data is stored in a local SQLite database for the purpose of the mini‑project. We retain records for audit and evaluation. If you deactivate your account, your data is preserved but not used for matching.

5. User Rights

You can update your personal information through your profile. You may deactivate your account at any time. To request complete deletion or further assistance, contact support.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact our administrators via the Support page.
