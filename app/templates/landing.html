How ASPEN Works

1. Farmers List Their Harvest

Farmers enter crop details, quantity, and spoilage time via a simple interface.

2. Vendors Place Orders

Restaurants, processors, and retailers specify their needs and minimum shelf life.

3. ASPEN Matches Them

Our engine prioritizes urgent produce and finds the closest feasible vendor automatically.
